Exercise 3: Lion Family and Friend

A father lion is a big lion moving from left to right corner of the frame. Although he is a powerful animal, he is a cute and shy person. A baby lion, always active. He moves around, bouncing every position of a canvas. Thomas, a dog character, is a character I borrowed from Sean. He is a cute dog and moves randomly out of the canvas.

I enjoyed doing this exercise; it is like a random action appearing in online animations that I have seen. The success of the work is the wrapping around and bouncing movement and the application of child and parent functions. Besides, I also use a vector of wrap or bounce code to control Thomas's creature.
