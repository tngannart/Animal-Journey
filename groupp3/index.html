Mini Project 3: Fruit Basket

Our group chose the theme is still life and concentrated on fruits in a 20x20 array. I do a pineapple and a grape, and Aron works on an apple, and Ryan does a blueberry. We based on the fruits and colored them similar to real images. This was a fun project, we could freely create images for them based on pixel art.
