Exercise 5: Animal Journey

I used p5play to work on this exercise. This is an interactive game with two scenes: dive into the ocean and feed the bunny. Diving into the ocean is the key to moving the crab to find the ocean's treasure. The feed bunny scene is used by pressing the mouse to feed the bunny. ending with the chewing sound, and the next scene will be shown.

How to play

Diving into the ocean:
+ A for turning left
+ D for turning right
+ W for moving up
+ S for moving down

Feeding bunny
+ Moving the mouse and clicking to let the bunny eat

Let's enjoy the game!
