Exercise 2: A Chess Board

This interactive piece explores movement and the combination of fonts and images in code. This is the first time I have combined keyPress() and other functions to create a complete work. My difficulty is that I have to make everything work as I want. I had many ideas, but I had to change them to fit the requirements. I did not have a good grasp of toggle, but after asking my classmates, I applied the toggle correctly. My success was creating a final for this exercise, I taught myself and added collision so that the two images touched each other and interacted to make it more impressive and interesting.

How to Interact

w = up

a = left

s = down

d = right

u = reset

x = on or off toggle

chess pieces collide = "Boom!"
